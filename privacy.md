# Privacy Policy (ARCA Drive Sync)

_Last updated: 2026-09-22_

ARCA Drive Sync is a personal, single-user utility.

**What it accesses:** files the owner explicitly places in a local sync
folder, and the owner's own Google Drive, via the Google Drive API.

**What it stores:** those files, in the owner's own Google Drive. Nothing
else is collected, stored or processed.

**Third parties:** none. No analytics, advertising, or data sale. The only
external services contacted are Google APIs required to perform the sync.
